1. Extract this folder from the zip first (right-click > Extract All). The sounds do not play inside a zip.
2. Open index.html in a web browser (Chrome, Edge or Firefox).
3. Enter your initials or a code name and answer every item, about 30 minutes in one sitting.
   Closing the page clears your answers.
4. Click 'Download my ratings (CSV)' and send the CSV file back.

Please rate on your own, and do not look up the project or discuss it with other raters.
